The safety checkpoint

Every child leaves with the right person.

When it's time to go, the app shows exactly who may collect each child — so no one is released by mistake.

  • ✓Each child has their own authorized pickup list (with relationships).
  • ✓A shared pool of volunteers and staff who can collect any child.
  • ✓Anyone not on the list is flagged in the record — nothing slips by unnoticed.
  • ✓Kids cleared to leave on their own are clearly marked “may leave alone.”
